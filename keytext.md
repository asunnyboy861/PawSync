## Description
PawSync is the all-in-one pet health manager that keeps your furry family members happy and healthy. Whether you have one pet or a whole pack, PawSync makes it easy to stay on top of vaccinations, medications, weight trends, and overall wellness.

SMART VACCINATION TRACKER
Never miss a shot again. PawSync tracks complete immunization histories for every pet with automatic reminders for upcoming and overdue vaccines. See your pet's full vaccination timeline at a glance and share records with your vet.

MEDICATION REMINDERS
Stay on top of every dose. Set custom reminders for daily medications, supplements, and treatments. Track dosage, frequency, and whether to take with food. Check off doses as given and maintain a complete medication history.

WEIGHT AND HEALTH MONITORING
Track your pet's weight over time with beautiful charts and trend analysis. Monitor changes that could signal health issues early. Record health events, symptoms, and vet observations all in one place.

AI HEALTH ASSISTANT
Get instant answers about your pet's symptoms, nutrition, and care tips powered by advanced AI. Ask questions anytime, day or night, and get reliable guidance to help you make informed decisions about your pet's health.

COMPLETE HEALTH SCORES
PawSync calculates a comprehensive health score for each pet based on vaccination status, medication adherence, weight trends, and overall care patterns. See at a glance how your pet is doing and where attention is needed.

MULTI-PET SUPPORT
Manage unlimited pets with individual profiles. Track species-specific needs for dogs, cats, and more. Each pet gets their own dashboard with tailored health insights.
